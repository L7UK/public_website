The definition

What is Defence Cyber Certification?

DCC is an organisation-wide cyber security certification developed by the Ministry of Defence with IASME.

It gives independent assurance that a supplier has implemented the controls required by Defence Standard 05-138 (Issue 4) at the level it is certified to. One certificate covers your organisation and can be presented across multiple defence procurements, rather than completing a separate assessment for each contract.

The controls align with relevant parts of the NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework, with Cyber Essentials at the core. Every level starts with Cyber Essentials, Levels 2 and 3 require Cyber Essentials Plus, and no level is a self-assessment.

The levels

Four levels, set by the risk of the work

The MOD or your prime contractor sets the level you need, based on the cyber risk profile of the contract. Certifying at a higher level satisfies the requirements of the levels below it.

  1. Level 0

    3 controls

    Very low cyber risk

    Basic cyber security practices. The foundation for every higher level.

    We assess and certify

  2. Level 1

    101 controls

    Low to moderate cyber risk

    A comprehensive cyber security programme with good practices.

  3. Level 2

    139 controls

    High cyber risk

    Advanced cyber security oversight and planning. Requires Cyber Essentials Plus.

  4. Level 3

    144 controls

    Substantial cyber risk

    Expert, defence-in-depth capability against new and evolving threats. Requires Cyber Essentials Plus.

Level 0

Three controls, one baseline

Level 0 is the minimum the MOD expects. To pass, every control must be fully met: there is no partial credit at this level.

The engagement

From scope to certificate

One Layer 7 assessor works with you from the first call to the certificate.

  1. 1

    Scoping

    We agree your applicant, certification boundary and the essential functions and services that must stay secure. We challenge the scope so it is logical and clearly documented.

  2. 2

    Cyber Essentials alignment

    We check your Cyber Essentials certificate covers the same scope. If it does not, or you do not hold one yet, we certify you first.

  3. 3

    Guidance

    We explain each control, the questions behind it and the evidence the assessor will expect. You prepare your responses and evidence; as your Certification Body we can guide, but we cannot write them for you.

  4. 4

    Assessment

    Our assessor marks your submission against the controls and verifies that they operate as described, remotely or on site. Clarification rounds let you respond to any questions.

  5. 5

    Certificate

    We issue your DCC certificate and you are listed on the IASME public registry. It is valid for three years, with an annual attestation and annual Cyber Essentials renewal.

Common questions

DCC FAQs

Is DCC mandatory?

IASME describes DCC as not yet universally mandatory. However, the MOD has asked all defence industry partners to achieve Level 0 by 31 December 2026, and higher levels are set contract by contract.

Which level do we need?

The MOD or your prime contractor assigns the level based on the cyber risk profile of the work. Most suppliers start at Level 0.

Do we need Cyber Essentials first?

Yes. Every DCC level starts with Cyber Essentials, and Levels 2 and 3 require Cyber Essentials Plus. Its scope must align with your DCC scope. We can certify both.

Is Level 0 a self-assessment?

No. No DCC level is a self-assessment. A Certification Body reviews your evidence and verifies the controls.

What does it take to pass Level 0?

All three controls must be fully met. Higher levels score each control and require at least 80% of the points in every objective.

Can you help us prepare?

Yes, within the scheme rules. We can explain the controls, clarify the questions, describe the evidence needed and verify your scope. We cannot implement changes or prepare the answers we later assess; for hands-on implementation you can use a separate provider.

How long does the certificate last?

Three years, with an annual attestation and annual renewal of Cyber Essentials in between.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is fixed once scope is agreed.
